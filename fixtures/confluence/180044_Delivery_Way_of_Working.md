## Delivery Process

The delivery process has four stages: planning, build, test, and release. Each stage has
an entry and exit checklist. Releases are cut on a two-week cadence.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The DM plugin and the adaptor are owned by the Platform team; the Journey plugin is owned
by the Engagement team. The MDC OTP Service is owned by the Security team. Cross-team
changes require sign-off from both owning teams.
