## Capacity Planning

Forecast monthly message volume and reserve capacity at the 95th percentile of the last
12 months. Capacity reviews happen quarterly. This page is about planning numbers, not
component configuration.

## Peak Handling

For seasonal peaks, pre-scale workers a week ahead. Note: this page intentionally does not
cover plugin-level settings such as batch sizes or retries — those live on the component
pages.
